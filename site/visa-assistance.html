Before you apply

Visa decisions are made solely by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority. Apex Travels provides assistance with preparation and submission and cannot guarantee approval, processing times or fees set by authorities. Please apply well ahead of your travel date and avoid booking non-refundable travel before the visa is issued.
